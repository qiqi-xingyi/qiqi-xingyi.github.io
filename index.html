Research statement

I am jointly advised by Dr. Qiang Guan at Kent State University and Dr. Feixiong Cheng at Cleveland Clinic.

In 2023, I was selected for the Quantum Talents Climbing Program at SZIQA, where I was mentored by Dr. Juan Yao.

My research spans two complementary directions. On the quantum side, I study algorithms, architectures, and systems. On the biological side, I work on virtual screening (VS), structure prediction, and drug discovery.

Current projects include protein binding-site prediction, molecular docking, structure reconstruction, and Hamiltonian compression.
